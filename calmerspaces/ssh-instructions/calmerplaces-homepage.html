Our standard

Verified means we checked. Every time.

Anyone can put “autism-friendly” on a website. We only list places where we’ve confirmed what they actually offer — so you’re not finding out on arrival that it wasn’t what you hoped.

  • Real programming, confirmed. We check for specific accommodations — hours, rooms, policies — not marketing language.
  • IBCCES certifications flagged clearly. Where venues hold Certified Autism Center status, we surface it prominently.
  • Specific, not vague. “Sensory hours every first Sunday 8–10am” — not “sensory accommodations available.”
  • Written by people who understand. Clear language, respectful framing, no clinical jargon.
