# 📧 Provider Outreach & Monetization Strategy (Week 1)

Now that we have **643 providers** live, the focus is on activating these listings and converting them into "claimed" or "paid" partners.

## 🎯 Target Segments

### Group A: High Priority (50–100 clinics)
*   **Attributes**: Clinics with specific pricing already scraped, "Allergan Diamond" status, high Google ratings (4.8+), or multiple locations.
*   **Goal**: Get 5-10 claimed listings in the first 48 hours.

### Group B: Baseline (Remaining 500+)
*   **Attributes**: Basic verified listings.
*   **Goal**: General awareness and passive claiming through the "Claim Listing" button.

## 🏗️ The "Value Prop" Story

We aren't just another Yelp or RealSelf. Our core value is **Extreme Transparency**:
1.  **Real Pricing**: We scrape and verify per-unit costs so users don't have to guess.
2.  **No Pay-to-Play**: Rankings are based on transparency and credentials, not who pays the most.
3.  **Injector Recognition**: We highlight "Elite" status (Allergan Black Diamond, etc.) automatically.

## 📈 Conversion Funnel Benchmarks
*   **Traffic Goal**: 500–2,000 sessions (Week 1 organic ramp).
*   **Lead Expectation**: 10–50 submissions (Target: 2-3% conversion rate).
*   **Claim Goal**: 2–5 verified claims per week.

---

## 🛠️ Activation Workflow

1.  **Lead Received**: When a lead hits for [City], route it to the **top 3 verified/claimed providers** first.
2.  **Notification**: Send the text/email notification (set up in the system).
3.  **The "Hook"**: After a provider receives their first free lead, follow up:
    > "Hi [Name], you just received a new patient lead for [Treatment] from InjectorIndex. How was the experience? Would you like to keep these coming?"
4.  **Social Proof**: Ask for a testimonial or a unique 'Clinic Special' to add to their profile.
